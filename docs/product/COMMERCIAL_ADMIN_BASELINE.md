# BCOS — Commercial & Administrative Product Baseline

> "Quem pede um, pede bis."

**Status:** PRODUCT GAP BASELINE / IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORITY FOR THE NEXT PRODUCT EXPANSION

## Product mission

BCOS is a multi-tenant SaaS sold and operated by **HPTECH PLATFORM** for coworking businesses. The product must serve three distinct business actors without mixing their data or authority:

1. **HPTECH / contracted service provider** — operates the SaaS, onboards contracting companies and administers platform access.
2. **Contracting coworking company (tenant)** — operates its own units, resources, professionals, pricing, bookings, usage and administrative finance.
3. **Professional / customer of the coworking** — discovers available spaces, prices, dates and times; books; follows usage; and sees/pays own charges.

Existing Booking, Usage, ResourceOccupancy, Pricing, Billing, Payments, Outbox and tenant isolation remain preserved. This baseline expands the product around those engines; it does not replace them.

## Required product surfaces

### A. HPTECH Platform Administration

A protected HPTECH backoffice must provide:

- contracting-company onboarding;
- tenant legal/trade identity and contact data;
- tenant activation, suspension and lifecycle state;
- initial OWNER administrator invitation;
- contracted SaaS plan/subscription association;
- enabled units/features;
- platform-level customer list and customer detail;
- operational onboarding status;
- separation between HPTECH financial administration and each tenant's internal finance.

HPTECH platform operators are not tenant OWNER users. Platform authority must be explicit and auditable.

### B. Identity, login and user lifecycle

Production identity must replace the homologation environment-token UX.

Required journeys:

- HPTECH administrator login;
- contracting-company OWNER first-access invitation and password/session setup;
- tenant OWNER/ADMIN user invitation and administration;
- RECEPTION user access;
- PROFESSIONAL invitation/activation;
- password reset;
- logout/revocation;
- tenant selection when one identity belongs to more than one tenant;
- server-authoritative role and tenant enforcement.

No browser-only role authority is accepted.

### C. Contracting-company administration

OWNER/ADMIN must be able to maintain:

- company profile;
- units/locations and timezone;
- reception/business hours;
- resource categories;
- physical resources: rooms, chairs, manicure stations and future rentable resource types;
- operational status and blocks/maintenance;
- professionals;
- tenant users and roles;
- pricing/rental rules;
- financial configuration.

### D. Professional administration

Tenant administration must support professional registration and lifecycle with at least:

- name and contact information;
- active/inactive status;
- identity/user binding when portal access is enabled;
- access to own-scope portal;
- association with bookings/usages/invoices without breaking existing tenant isolation.

### E. Commercial availability and calendar booking

The professional booking experience must not require guessing a date/time in a raw form.

It must expose a calendar/search journey that can answer, before booking:

- which unit;
- which resource/resource category;
- which dates;
- which time slots;
- which spaces are available;
- applicable price/rental condition;
- booking duration/window;
- unavailable/blocked periods.

Canonical availability remains ResourceOccupancy/domain authority. Pricing remains Pricing authority. The UI consumes these authorities and must not recreate conflict or pricing rules client-side.

Target journey:

`Professional → choose unit/resource type → calendar → available day → available time/resource + price → review → booking → confirmation`.

Reception may create bookings administratively, but that is not a substitute for the professional discovery/booking journey.

### F. Administrative finance

Existing Billing/Invoice/Payment remains the operational receivable engine generated by usage. Add an administrative finance boundary instead of overloading Invoice.

Tenant-scoped finance requires:

- bank/financial accounts;
- cash registers (cash money);
- payment/receipt methods;
- payment/receipt terms;
- accounts receivable;
- accounts payable;
- settlement/reversal history;
- financial movements;
- cash-flow projection and realized cash flow;
- chart of accounts for inflows/outflows;
- cost categories / cost plan;
- linkage from BCOS invoices/payments into financial movements without duplicating billing authority;
- audit trail.

HPTECH finance and tenant finance are separate scopes. A tenant can never read or mutate HPTECH or another tenant's financial records.

## Role/surface matrix

| Surface | HPTECH operator | Tenant OWNER | Tenant ADMIN | Reception | Professional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HPTECH customer administration | yes | no | no | no | no |
| Tenant company configuration | support/audited only | yes | yes | limited | no |
| Units/resources/pricing | support/audited only | yes | yes | operational subset | read availability |
| Users/professionals | support/audited only | yes | yes | limited | own profile |
| Booking administration | support/audited only | yes | yes | yes | own bookings |
| Commercial availability/calendar | support/audited only | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Tenant finance | support/audited only | yes | configurable | configurable | own charges only |
| HPTECH finance | yes | no | no | no | no |

"support/audited only" does not grant silent tenant impersonation. Any future support-access mechanism requires explicit authority and audit semantics.

## Delivery gates

### Gate C1 — Product authority and identity
- platform-operator authority;
- production identity/session contract;
- tenant lifecycle/onboarding;
- invitation and user lifecycle.

### Gate C2 — Tenant master data
- company profile;
- units/hours;
- categories/resources;
- professionals/users;
- administration UI.

### Gate C3 — Pricing and commercial calendar
- pricing administration;
- calendar availability query/read model;
- professional space/time/price discovery;
- booking review/confirmation.

### Gate C4 — Administrative finance foundation
- financial accounts and cash registers;
- methods/terms;
- chart of accounts and costs;
- payables/receivables;
- movements and settlements.

### Gate C5 — Finance views
- cash flow;
- operational Billing integration;
- reconciliation and audit views.

### Gate C6 — Role-specific product shells
- HPTECH console;
- tenant owner/admin console;
- reception workspace;
- professional portal.

### Gate C7 — Production E2E and human homologation
- HPTECH creates/onboards tenant;
- OWNER activates access;
- tenant configures unit/resources/prices/users;
- professional activates access;
- professional discovers availability and price;
- professional books;
- reception check-in/use/check-out;
- Billing materializes receivable;
- payment/receipt updates finance;
- tenant sees cash/receivable state;
- tenant isolation and role boundaries verified.

## Non-negotiable acceptance rule

BCOS is not launch-ready merely because the operational booking lifecycle works. Launch readiness requires the complete business journey from **HPTECH onboarding → tenant configuration → professional self-service discovery/booking → operation → financial control**, with production identity and tenant isolation proven.

Already reusable:
